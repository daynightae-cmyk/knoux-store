About Me

Creative mind,
technical soul

I'm Alex Rivera, a multi-disciplinary creative director and 3D artist based in Milan, with over a decade of experience crafting stunning visual narratives for global brands, record labels, and luxury fashion houses.

My work lives at the intersection of art and technology — where immersive 3D environments meet bold graphic design and cinematic storytelling. I believe great design isn't just seen; it's felt.

From campaign ideation to final delivery, I bring an obsessive attention to detail and a relentless pursuit of the unexpected.

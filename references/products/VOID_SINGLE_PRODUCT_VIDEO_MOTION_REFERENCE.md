## Visual observations
The recording repeatedly demonstrates a persistent black cinematic field where:
- a dense particle object forms/dissolves in place
- thin orbit/ring geometry frames the focal object
- large editorial text remains sparse and offset from the 3D focal object
- ring/tunnel geometry grows to fill the viewport during traversal
- section content changes while the same visual world persists
- later states introduce grounded spatial planes and a large concluding statement
- the visual hierarchy is object first, text second, controls tertiary
- large negative space remains throughout

## Asset and mechanics

- `public/founder/sadek-elgazar.jpg` is the original `assets/254654069.jpg`, copied byte for byte (460 × 460, 33,989 bytes). SHA-256: `03802b2810bc6298eec9b1ed11ea61998e569389ab9e5d3e7e2eda974cd60b50`.
- `src/lib/founderPortrait.ts` adapts the recovered `particle-portrait.js`: image luminance, Sobel edges, face-weighted sample density, dispersed point assembly over 2.2 seconds, breathing and desktop pointer repulsion. The portrait is sampled from the real photo; no replacement likeness is generated.
- Palette uses silver/off-white and muted lavender for the dark portrait contours, preserving the source's luminance distinction without its saturated violet. CSS grayscale presents the original yellow photo within the current institutional palette without altering the asset bytes.
- Sampling is deterministic and capped at 5,500 desktop/tablet or 2,000 coarse-pointer particles; DPR is capped at 2 or 1.5. Canvas drawing runs at most 30fps. Intersection, visibility and preference listeners stop animation outside the viewport, in hidden tabs or with reduced motion. Reduced motion produces a complete, stationary portrait. Cleanup removes RAF, observers and listeners.
- Initial visibility is derived from current geometry on load/resize; the observer watches the stable frame. Passive scroll events coalesce one geometry check per frame, covering delayed observer delivery during smooth/hash scrolling without an idle offscreen loop. A reproduced desktop lifecycle failure had retained an initial offscreen entry despite a visible tab and a fully intersecting canvas. The fix prevents that stale entry from parking the portrait and stops drawing when scrolling away. The same motion/offscreen/resume test is retained, with failure diagnostics attached; four focused desktop repetitions passed after the fix.
- A further reproduced failure had the browser's reduced-motion preference enabled while drawing continued. Each animation callback now validates its generation and the current motion/visibility preference before drawing, preventing queued callbacks from reviving a stopped loop. Four focused desktop repetitions passed with assembly, offscreen pause/resume and a frozen reduced-motion draw counter.
- The photograph is SSR-visible and lazy-loaded. Successful particle sampling retains a faint grayscale photo underlayer (24% opacity; 35% on coarse pointers), so the face remains recognizable with the bounded mobile particle density. Unsupported canvas/sampling retains the full photograph. The photograph toggle also stops all particle work and provides a native keyboard control. Founder text and links do not depend on canvas.
